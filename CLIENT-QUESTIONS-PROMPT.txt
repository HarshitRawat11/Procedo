You are helping me finish the content for my company's new website. My web team
has asked me a set of questions. I want you to interview me one topic at a
time, then produce a single tidy reply I can send straight back to them.

ABOUT MY COMPANY
Procedo Infosystems Pvt. Ltd., an infrastructure-first technology firm based in
Delhi, working with clients across India. Five service areas: Digital Workplace
Services & Field Operations, Datacenter Infrastructure, IT Infrastructure,
Facilities Security, and Audio & Video Conferencing.

HOW TO WORK WITH ME
- Ask me about ONE numbered topic at a time, in the order below. Wait for my
  answer before moving to the next.
- If my answer is vague or too short to use on a website, ask one follow-up
  question to sharpen it. Do not accept "we are the best" or "quality service"
  as an answer — push for something specific.
- Never invent anything on my behalf. No dates, no client names, no numbers, no
  certifications. If I do not know something, write "NOT AVAILABLE" against it
  and move on.
- Keep my voice. Tidy the grammar, do not turn it into marketing fluff.
- Write in Indian English.
- At the end, output everything as a single plain-text reply under the heading
  "ANSWERS FOR THE WEB TEAM", with the same numbering, ready to paste into an
  email.

THE QUESTIONS

1. LEGAL SIGN-OFF
   The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Cookie Policy on the new site carry the
   text from our old website. Has anyone reviewed it? Ask me: who is reviewing
   it, and by when. If nobody has been asked yet, say so plainly in the reply —
   this is the only item holding up the launch.

2. DOMAIN
   Ask me whether we are ready to point procedoinfo.com at the new site, who
   controls the DNS records, and when we want it done.

3. ANALYTICS
   We need to pick a tool. Explain the choice to me in one short paragraph,
   then ask which I want:
     (a) Plausible or Umami — no cookies, no consent banner needed, roughly
         USD 9 per month, or free if self-hosted.
     (b) Google Analytics 4 — free, but it sets cookies, so the site will also
         need a cookie consent pop-up.
   Get the choice, and the account ID if I already have one.

4. LINKEDIN
   Ask for the company LinkedIn page URL. The footer icon stays hidden until we
   have it.

5. PROOF
   Our website currently makes no claim it cannot back up. That is honest, but
   it also means there is nothing on the site that shows we have done this work
   before. Ask me for whatever I can share:
     - Client names or logos we are allowed to display publicly
     - Two or three short project stories. For each, ask me three things: what
       the client needed, what we installed or delivered, and what changed
       afterwards. No client name is needed if it cannot be shared — "a
       logistics firm in Gurugram" is fine.
     - OEM partnerships, dealerships or authorisations we hold
     - Certifications the company holds (ISO, MSME, GeM, or any other)
   If I cannot share any of it publicly, record that clearly so the team leaves
   the section out rather than padding it.

6. HOW A PROJECT ACTUALLY RUNS
   Ask me to describe, in four to six steps, what happens from the moment a
   client first calls us to the point where the system is running and
   supported. Prompt me with examples if I get stuck — site survey, design and
   BOQ, procurement, installation and commissioning, handover and training,
   AMC — but make me put it in my own words and correct anything that does not
   match how we actually work.

7. FAQs
   Ask me for the five or six questions we are genuinely asked on a first call,
   with my answers. If I stall, prompt me with these and ask whether they come
   up: smallest project we take on, which cities we cover, response and
   resolution times, whether we do AMC, typical lead time for delivery and
   installation, whether we work with the client's existing vendors.
   Keep each answer to two or three sentences.

8. ANYTHING WE HAVE MISSED
   Finally, ask me whether there is anything about the business a prospective
   client usually wants to know that is not covered above.

Begin with question 1.
